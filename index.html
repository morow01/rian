07:40Before you leave the yard

Plan the run from the map.

Every routine site you look after is a pin, coloured by how long it has been.

  • Green, amber, red, grey — visited this month, due soon, overdue, never visited.
  • Show my location to see where you are against the list — real GPS in the Android app.
  • Tap a pin for the last visit, then Log Visit or open Google Maps directions.
09:15Outside the exchange

Know the site before you open the door.

Search every exchange and cabinet by name or code — no signal-hungry directory hunt.

  • Address, coordinates, power supply, security and emergency contacts in one place.
  • Separate Exchanges and Cabinets tabs, each with its own search.
  • On desktop: results on the left, details and an embedded Google Map on the right.
10:30Hands dirty

Write it down while it's fresh.

Faults, follow-ups and job reminders — searchable, filterable and never lost between devices.

  • Photos and files — pick from the camera roll (auto-compressed, uploaded to the cloud) or attach a PDF or spreadsheet.
  • Dictate instead of typing, and let AI writing assist offer up to three rewrites of a rushed description.
  • Reopen a note and you're back exactly where you stopped — scroll and cursor included.
  • Reminders arrive as push notifications, even with the app closed.
11:20Lunch in the van

A notebook per project In development

Notebooks, sections and pages with a full rich-text editor — tables, images and collapsible blocks. Journal is still under active development: it works today and keeps growing.

  • Voice-to-table: say “24 cells, cell 1 is 12.4 volts…” and get a finished battery table.
  • Colour-coded sections, copy or move pages, and right-click menus on desktop.
22:40Phone buzzes

A fault call after hours, logged in a minute.

Your on-call record stays accurate without a spreadsheet at midnight.

  • Paste the Remedy ticket — site, fault and date are filled in for you.
  • Log each incident against its on-call week; stats build over time.
  • Edit the schedule in one pass: cells cycle blank → on-call → extra, with a single confirm.
  • Upcoming and Previous weeks sit behind one tidy toggle.
FRI 16:00Close out the week

Ordinary, overtime, done.

Log hours against work codes as you go; on Friday the week is already summed.

  • Ordinary and overtime per task, with codes and locations attached.
  • Task Templates — save a code-and-hours combination once, insert it in one tap.
  • Weekly Summary with percentage bars; one tap emails it, callouts included.
LATER“Didn't we fix this before?”

Find that fault again.

Search every saved week, task and callout, then jump to the exact record.

  • Highlighted matches with filters for All, Tasks or Callouts.
  • On a phone, results sit under a heading for each week, so you can see when it happened.
  • The AI Fault Assistant answers across notes, task notes and timesheets — by voice if you like.
