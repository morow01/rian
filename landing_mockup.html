01 — Timesheet

Log daily tasks, work codes and hours

Record ordinary and overtime hours against specific work codes and site locations for each day of the week.

  • Day-by-day task logging Expand any day to enter tasks, attach work codes, select site locations, and add notes.
  • Ordinary & overtime tracking Separately enter ORD and OT hours per task with automatic week totals.
  • Weekly email & export Generate a formatted summary email of your week's hours to send to supervisor or payroll.
  • Templates & bulk actions Save recurring daily task templates, duplicate entries, or bulk copy tasks across days.
  • Availability Planner Track leave, training, CTO shifts, and on-call rosters with badges visible in the timesheet header.
02 — Notes

Site notes, reminders and follow-ups

Keep track of job details, faults, and meeting points. Group by category or custom tag, set due dates, and convert notes into timesheet tasks.

  • 6 categories & custom tags Organise notes under Job, Fault, Follow-up, Meeting, Personal, or Idea, plus your own custom tag list.
  • Priority flags & tabs Filter by High, Medium, Low priority, or switch between Active, Archive, and Bin views.
  • Due dates & reminders Set reminder dates and receive notifications when follow-ups are due.
  • Voice dictation Dictate notes on site using speech-to-text when your hands are busy.
  • Convert note to timesheet task Create a task on your current week's timesheet directly from any note.
03 — Journal

Structured notebooks & battery table recorder

Maintain technical documentation across Notebooks, Sections, and Pages with rich text editing, tables, Word document import, and AI-assisted voice table entry.

  • Notebooks → Sections → Pages Organise long-term documentation by project, site area, and visit date.
  • TipTap rich text editor Headings, bullet lists, formatting, images, and data tables built right into each page.
  • Battery voice table recorder Dictate cell voltage readings sequentially. Gemini AI structures the numbers and inserts a clean table into your page.
  • DOCX Word import Import existing Word documents directly into Journal pages without retyping.
04 — Finder

Site directory & Google Maps navigation

Search exchanges and street cabinets by town name or site code, view address coordinates, and launch directions with one tap.

  • Instant search Quickly filter through exchanges and cabinets by site code or location name.
  • Google Maps navigation Tap the location to open turn-by-turn directions straight to the site.
  • Exchanges & Cabinets Dedicated search tabs for main telephone exchanges and roadside cabinets.
  • Desktop two-panel layout Search results list on the left with site details and map preview on the right.
05 — Routines

Monthly routine visit schedule

Track recurring site maintenance month by month. The app automatically detects routine visits from your timesheets so you never have to log them twice.

  • Auto-detected from timesheet Site visits on your timesheet automatically populate your monthly routine grid and receive an AUTO tag.
  • Site selector & year switcher Choose which sites you maintain and switch between years from the header subtitle.
  • Visit stats & overdue alerts View monthly visit counts, sites overdue by 3+ months, and sites not yet visited this year.
  • Routine map view Full-width tab switcher (Grid | Stats | Map) to inspect site locations geographically.
06 — Callouts

On-call records & Remedy ticket parsing

Record standby weeks and log emergency callout incidents with ticket numbers, site names, fault details, and actions taken.

  • Paste Remedy tickets Paste raw fault text directly — site, fault description, and date are parsed automatically into a new callout.
  • On-call schedule & stats Track on-call weeks, extra shifts, total incidents, and average callouts per week.
  • Included in timesheet export Callout incidents for the active week automatically attach to your weekly timesheet export email.
  • Desktop three-panel layout Weeks list on the left, callouts in the middle, and full incident form on the right.
07 — History & AI

Search past records & ask technical questions

Find old jobs across past weeks, jump to dates with the calendar picker, and ask the built-in Gemini assistant questions about your notes and records.

  • Week History search Search across all saved tasks and callouts with highlighted matching snippets.
  • Jump to exact record Tap any search result to open the matching day's timesheet or callout incident directly.
  • AI Fault Assistant Ask questions referencing your saved notes, timesheets, and technical records.
  • Data protection & backups Automatic weekly snapshots, offline cache in IndexedDB, and visual conflict resolution.
08 — Desktop Mode

Purpose-built three-panel layout for laptops

When opened on screens 1280px or wider, Rian switches to a dedicated desktop layout designed for fast review and typing.

  • Days · Tasks · Detail panels Select a day on the left, a task in the center, and edit details and work codes on the right.
  • Adapted desktop views across all tabs Timesheet, Notes, Journal, Finder, Routines, and Callouts each feature dedicated desktop layouts.
  • Custom tab pinning & shortcuts Pin your most-used tabs directly to the header bar. Universal Escape key closes open modals.
