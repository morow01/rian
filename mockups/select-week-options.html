Why this solves the exact challenge Perfect Synergy

Since past weeks are already in the main Week History page, this design cleanly addresses the two distinct future use cases:

  • 1
    Near Future (1–6 Weeks) via "Upcoming" Tab:
    When entering upcoming events next week or 2–4 weeks out, a list is the fastest: 1 tap without thinking about days or months.
  • 2
    Far Future (2–12 Months Ahead) via "Calendar" Tab:
    A stream cannot realistically list 52 weeks without endless scrolling. The Month Calendar allows jumping to November, December, or next year in seconds.
  • 3
    True Friday–Thursday Boundary:
    Instead of a standard picker where selecting a Tuesday is confusing, tapping any day highlights the whole Friday-to-Thursday timesheet block so you always know which timesheet week you are opening.
  • 4
    Reuses Existing Callouts Component Pattern:
    Matches the Upcoming | Previous segmented tab look already in Rian's Callouts header.
