The Unified Solution

Now both Week History and Callouts share the exact same intuitive hierarchy:

  • 1
    "THIS WEEK" Stays Anchored at the Top:
    The active timesheet week is immediately visible with hours, OT, and task count badge.
  • 2
    "Previous" | "Upcoming" Segmented Toggle:
    Directly below the current week. Tapping Previous (19) shows past saved weeks; tapping Upcoming (4) shows upcoming planned weeks.
  • 3
    Top Header Calendar Icon:
    Tapping the calendar icon in the top header opens the Visual Month Calendar so the user can easily jump to any month/year across the entire calendar with Friday-to-Thursday week stripe highlights.
