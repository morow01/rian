Theme chooser — options

Each frame is the ☰ → Display → Theme block at phone width. All five are clickable. Colours are pulled from the real theme tokens (page, header, card, accent) of the six live themes: Light, Dark, Champagne, Champagne Dark, macOS, LCD.
