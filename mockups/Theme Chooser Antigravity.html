Antigravity Concept 1

Aura Swatches

A compact 3×2 grid of tactile tokens. Each token features a dual-tone circular disk (showing the canvas & card relationship) centered with a glowing accent diode.

✓ Highlights

  • Ultra-clean 3×2 layout that takes minimal vertical height.
  • Zero border clipping or awkward stripe seams.
  • Concentric circular diodes feel like precision hardware switches.

💡 Ergonomics

  • Large, accessible tap targets (48px+ touch area).
  • Instantly previews page canvas, card tone, and accent highlight.

Antigravity Concept 2

Split-Prism Cards

Refines the 2-column structure by replacing the 3 vertical stripes with a 120° angled geometric facet and an anchored accent badge. Includes helpful subtitle tags for quick recognition.

✓ Highlights

  • Maintains 2-column muscle memory.
  • Angle cut prevents the pale stripe from looking like a border gap.
  • Subtitle tags clearly communicate the flavor of each theme.

💡 Ergonomics

  • Direct drop-in replacement in app.html.
